1. **Reuse the registered pipeline.** [`_pyhyper_iq`](../../../tests/validation/test_bragg_3d_lattice.py) already selects the case energy and passes it to the remesh. Reuse it wherever appropriate. Extend [the high-q evaluator](../fft_registration/bragg_high_q.py) and [reporter](../fft_registration/report_bragg_high_q.py) or reuse their helpers from the dev probe. Keep the existing signed-grating test in the Bragg suite and add the [padded predictions](ZP01-reference.md). Pair grating and lattice results in the reports. Check the raw detector coordinates and masks before radial reduction.
2. **Calculate D at the reported physical pixels.** Use `qz=−q_perp²/(k+√(k²−q_perp²))`, exact complex finite-voxel sums at these depths, and the transverse projection evaluated at the true outgoing direction. The [CPU pilot](CPU_PILOT.md) evaluates every in-band pixel and cross-checks one pixel in each band with a full XYZ sum. Extend D to indexed peak windows, local background and near-bin/between-bin groups. Keep geometric acceptance tied to `2π/(N d)` because the physical film thickness sets the reciprocal peak width. Padding refines the interpolation grid while leaving the specimen thickness and Nyquist bound fixed. At fixed `q_perp` the 570 eV Ewald surface is flatter, which makes the common band and the new outer band separate comparisons.
3. **Count only identified changes in peaks.** Record the common valid mask and each arm’s unsupported pixels. Match by projected spot identity and retain contributing `(h,k,l)` indices so nearly coincident HCP orders count once. Freeze predictor windows, annuli and thresholds using the established rules and D. Use the reporter’s three-pixel search, 4–9 pixel background annulus and existing HCP/SC contrast criteria. Sweep relative peak floors `10⁻³, 10⁻⁴, 10⁻⁵`. Reuse the direct (100) normalization for the maintained anchors. For BCC, (100) is systematically absent. Preselect and verify a strong, accessible allowed reflection from D, such as a member of {110}, for each orientation/energy. Record its index and use the same normalization for all M. Declare the BCC contrast rule from D before comparing A/C. Examine odd `h+k+l` locations for leakage using the finite-specimen calculation.
4. **Return interpretable evidence.** Report A/D, B/D and C/D band intensity relative L2 on common support, B/C parity on feasible full panels, and reference-confirmed, recovered, lost, false and missed spots at each floor. Include per-spot centroid residual, integrated intensity and width, with signed line cuts through one near-bin and one between-bin reflection. Report the per-order error when counts hold steady. Produce paired A, C and D maps on common scales and a depth-versus-error plot. Keep parent radial-label discrepancies identified separately in the reduced-output assessment.
